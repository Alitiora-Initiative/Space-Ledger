Treasury & Vesting

Transparency page for the proposed treasury design.

Not deployed

The treasury allocation, vesting schedule, and fee routing shown in early drafts are proposals only. There is no deployed contract to lock funds, collect fees, or release tokens. No vesting progress or balance is reported as fact.

Proposed parameters

Draft documents describe a 12-month cliff and linear vesting over 36 months. These values are not enforced on-chain and should not be treated as an active schedule.

Payment destination configuration

The backend has a wallet destination configured for future use. It does not make purchases, fee collection, or withdrawals active. The app will not request a wallet transaction while ownership and settlement remain undeployed.
