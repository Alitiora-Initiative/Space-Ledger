Treasury Allocation

1 billion blocks (10% of total supply) are allocated to the dev treasury at genesis. These blocks are locked in a smart-contract vault with a structured release schedule to prevent market dilution and build long-term trust.

Total Supply: 10,000,000,000 blocks

Treasury Allocation: 1,000,000,000 blocks (10%)

Public Allocation: 9,000,000,000 blocks (90%)

Vesting Schedule

Phase 1 - Cliff Period (12 months):

Zero token liquidity is released during the initial dev and launch phases. This prevents immediate selling and ensures long-term commitment.

Phase 2 - Linear Vesting (36 months):

Following the cliff, blocks vest linearly over 36 months, protecting against sudden token influxes and ensuring gradual, controlled release.

Automated Micro-Fee Collection

The platform funds ongoing development through an automated micro-fee built directly into smart contract logic. Every block transaction incurs a flat service fee of $0.10 USD, processed in stablecoins (USDC or USDT).

Fee per Transaction: $0.10 USD (flat fee)

Fee Currency: USDC / USDT

Destination: Smart-contract vault

Purpose: Ongoing development funding

Automatic Backend Processes

All vesting, cliff period enforcement, and fee collection are handled automatically by the backend smart contracts. No manual intervention is required.

Vesting: Automatic linear release after 12-month cliff

Fee Collection: Automatic $0.10 fee per transaction

Execution: Smart contract runs in background
