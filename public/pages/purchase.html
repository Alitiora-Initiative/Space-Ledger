How Pricing Works

P(x) = (9.999 × 10⁻¹⁰) × x + 0.001

Price starts at $0.001 per block and rises linearly to $10.00 at block 9,999,999,999. Early buyers get lower prices — as more blocks sell, the price increases automatically. A flat $0.10 platform fee is added per transaction and routed automatically — you never configure it.
