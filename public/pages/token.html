Why this coin exists

$SL was launched by the Space Ledger operator so people who want the website and protocol to keep moving can do it in public, on Solana mainnet. Proceeds the creator actually receives — trading-fee share from the StonkFun / Raydium LaunchLab curve, and any tokens the creator sells — are intended for development.

  • Hosting and production deploys for this site
  • Solana RPC so quotes, grid, and receipts stay fast
  • Interface, mobile, and wallet work
  • Security review before any mainnet canvas
  • Ongoing open-source work in the GitHub repository

That spending intent is not a smart-contract escrow. The token does not pay dividends, does not grant canvas blocks, and does not give equity in Alitiora Initiative. Devnet purchases on the rest of this site are still test tokens with no real-world value.

Read this before you buy

  • $SL can go to zero. A new bonding-curve coin is speculative.
  • This page is not financial, legal, or tax advice.
  • Space Ledger never asks for a seed phrase and does not execute the swap. Buys happen on StonkFun in your own wallet.
  • Market figures below are other people’s public APIs. If a source is down, that row says so instead of a made-up number.
  • Reported liquidity on a fresh curve is often tiny. Market cap is not cash you can withdraw.
