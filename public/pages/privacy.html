What we process

  • On your device: wallet connection state may be stored in session storage for reconnect in the same browser tab. No seed phrases or private keys are requested.
  • On our server (Vercel): API request metadata (IP for rate limits, timestamps), quote and purchase-build requests. Logs follow the host provider’s retention.
  • On Solana Devnet: public purchase receipts and program state. Blockchain data is public by design.
  • RPC providers: when configured, a third-party Devnet RPC (for example Helius) processes read/write RPC traffic under that provider’s terms.
