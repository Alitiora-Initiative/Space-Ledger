About Space Ledger

Space Ledger is a shared canvas of 10 billion blocks and 1 trillion pixels, inspired by the Million Dollar Homepage format. Each block represents a unique 10×10 pixel grid that can be owned and displayed on-chain.

Built on Solana's high-performance blockchain, Space Ledger uses a transparent bonding-curve pricing model starting at $0.001 per block and scaling to $1,000 as supply advances. All purchases are recorded on-chain with immutable receipts, ensuring verifiable ownership without centralized intermediaries.

Currently in beta on Solana Devnet, the platform demonstrates a scalable model for digital asset ownership and community-driven canvas curation. Test purchases use Devnet SOL and USDC—test tokens with no real-world value.

Key Features

  • Transparent Pricing: Linear bonding curve from $0.001 to $1,000 per block, mathematically predictable and fully on-chain
  • On-Chain Ownership: Every purchase recorded as an immutable Solana transaction receipt, verifiable by anyone
  • Real-Time Supply: Live Devnet data shows exact blocks sold, available inventory, and holder rankings
  • Portfolio Tracking: View your holdings, purchase history, and estimated model PnL in your profile
  • Grid Exploration: Interactive 10×10 pixel grid view for browsing and discovering available blocks
