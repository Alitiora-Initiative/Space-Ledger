About Space Ledger

Space Ledger is a shared canvas of 10 billion blocks and 1 trillion pixels, inspired by the Million Dollar Homepage's pixel-by-pixel format. The original launched in 2005. Space Ledger accepts test purchases on Solana Devnet and records block ranges in on-chain receipts. Connect a compatible Solana wallet on Devnet to request a quote, approve a purchase, and view confirmed holdings. Transfers, swaps, and resales are not enabled.

One trillion pixels, addressable in blocks

Each numbered block contains a 10 × 10 cluster of pixels. That makes a simple shared coordinate system: 10 billion blocks multiplied by 100 pixels per block gives 1 trillion pixel positions. The grid is designed for exploring that scale, while Devnet receipts currently record block ranges rather than artwork or transferable assets.

What is live in this beta

Supply, primary purchases, portfolio ranges, and holder rankings are read from confirmed Solana Devnet program accounts. A wallet must approve each test purchase. Devnet tokens are for testing only and have no real-world value.

Block ownership cannot currently be transferred or resold. There is no token pool, Jupiter route, or secondary trading feed, so the app does not display trading candles or claim a market price. The published curve is a purchase-price model.
