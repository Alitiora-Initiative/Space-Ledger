About Space Ledger

Space Ledger is an experimental shared canvas of 1 trillion pixels, divided into 10 billion 10×10 blocks. Inspired by The Million Dollar Homepage (launched in 2005), it explores how a classic pixel grid might work with wallet integrations and a blockchain-themed pricing model. This prototype does not currently verify block ownership on-chain.

Bonding Curve Pricing

P(x) = (9.99999999 × 10⁻⁸)x + 0.001

The price of each block follows a linear bonding curve, starting at $0.001 and reaching $1,000.00 for the final block. This is an experimental pricing model, not a promise of investment returns.
