About Space Ledger

Space Ledger is a shared canvas of 10 billion blocks and 1 trillion pixels, inspired by the Million Dollar Homepage's pixel-by-pixel format. The original launched in 2005. Space Ledger accepts test purchases on Solana Devnet and records block ranges in on-chain receipts. Connect Phantom on Devnet to see the current quote, approve a purchase, and view confirmed holdings. Vesting, swaps, and resales are not enabled.

Bonding Curve Pricing
