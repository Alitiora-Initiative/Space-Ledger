How to buy a block

  1. Connect once (Phantom or Solflare on Devnet).
  2. Open Grid or Pricing.
  3. Purchases settle as a whole block (100 pixels).
  4. Get a quote → Review and buy in wallet → approve.
  5. Check Explorer and Profile.

Devnet SOL/USDC have no real-world value.

How to test (Discord / free tier)

  1. Wallet on Devnet + free faucet SOL.
  2. Connect Wallet in the top nav.
  3. Pricing → budget → quote → approve.
  4. If the network is busy, wait a minute and retry (public free RPC).
