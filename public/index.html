About Space Ledger

Space Ledger is a 10 billion block, 1 trillion pixel canvas concept inspired by The Million Dollar Homepage (launched in 2005). The current site provides a live visual canvas, wallet connection, and a read-only estimate of its proposed pricing model. Purchases, ownership, treasury vesting, and resales are not live because no ownership contract or indexer has been deployed.
